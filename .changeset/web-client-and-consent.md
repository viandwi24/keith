Reach and address (P37): Keith knows whether you are at your desk, busy or out, holds normal messages while you are away and briefs you
once when you are back; persona addressing and onboarding in chat.

Web client (P38): the core serves a browser app at `/app` with the TUI's flow (sign-in with an HttpOnly cookie, chat, approvals and
step-up, Home and the orb, every Manage section, plugin views, a guest page), a phone layout, a PWA and Web Push.

Conversational consent (P39): what you ask for yourself runs at once and can be undone; risky actions are asked in the conversation and
answered with a plain yes or no; requests that follow outside content go to the Inbox and a trusted device.
